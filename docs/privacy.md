# hometty Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-27

hometty is a home screen launcher developed by luftaquila. It has no ads or hometty accounts. The app does not send your contacts, notifications, notes or shell history to a developer-operated server. Optional network features and Google Play billing communicate with the services described below.

**On the device**
- Settings, notes, aliases and shell history are stored in the app's private storage. Clearing app storage or uninstalling hometty removes that storage. Files you export or create outside it, including backups, must be deleted separately.
- With your permission, contacts are used for contact lookup and calls, and notifications are displayed and filtered in the terminal. hometty does not automatically upload this content.
- Permissions are optional and can be revoked in Android settings. Features that need a revoked permission will stop working.

**Network**
- Weather: with location permission, your approximate coordinates are sent over HTTPS to your selected weather service, [wttr.in](https://github.com/chubin/wttr.in) or [OpenWeather](https://openweather.co.uk/privacy-policy). OpenWeather also receives the API key you supply. Android's geocoding service may process the coordinates to obtain a place name. Disable the weather provider or revoke location permission to stop location-based weather requests.
- RSS: feeds are fetched over HTTPS from the addresses you add. Feed servers receive the request and your IP address.
- Shell commands, web searches, links and files you choose to share can send data to destinations you select. Their handling of that data is governed by those services. Weather, feed and other service providers may retain request information under their own policies; hometty does not control their retention.

**Payments**
- Optional donations are processed by Google Play. hometty receives purchase status and purchase tokens to complete transactions, but does not receive your card or bank details. Google Play's billing library also sends billing diagnostics to Google. Google handles this information under its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- The developer can access transaction reports provided by Play Console. Contact us about a purchase or use Google Play's purchase support tools.

**Contact**
- [dev@luftaquila.io](mailto:dev@luftaquila.io). If you email us, we receive the address and information you provide to respond to your request. You can also use this address for privacy and deletion requests concerning information held by the developer.
