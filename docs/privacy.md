# Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-27

hometty collects no personal data. It has no ads, analytics or accounts, and sends nothing to the developer.

**On the device**
- Settings, notes, aliases and everything else you enter stay on the device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
- Contacts and notifications are read only to show them in the app and never leave the device.

**Network**
- Weather: your approximate location is sent to the weather service you choose (wttr.in or OpenWeather) and to the device's geocoder for place names.
- RSS: feeds are fetched from the addresses you add.

**Payments**
- Donations are handled by Google Play. The app never sees your payment details.
